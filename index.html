Help

Press Start and allow camera access. Directions are from your own point of view.

  • Speech: left or right first, then up or down, then distance. Each can be "a little", plain, or "far". "Centered" means all good.
  • Sounds: blips pan left or right with your face, and go higher when your face is high, lower when it is low. Blips stop and a rising chime plays when you are centered. A falling tone means the face was lost.
  • Distance: if blips keep playing in the middle while you are centered, you are too close or too distant. Sit where you want to be and press "Set current distance as ideal".
  • Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+W says where you are. Alt+Shift+S starts or stops.
  • Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Video never leaves your computer.
